Medical Equipment Service Workflow

The exact procedure depends on the equipment and clinical situation, but a typical service intervention follows a structured and traceable workflow.

  1. 01
    Receive & Review

    Review the reported issue, equipment status, service history and relevant clinical context.

  2. 02
    Assess & Prioritize

    Evaluate technical risk and clinical urgency, and determine whether the equipment should remain out of service.

  3. 03
    Investigate

    Perform inspection, measurements and functional testing to identify the fault or maintenance requirement.

  4. 04
    Service

    Complete the required preventive maintenance, adjustment or corrective technical action.

  5. 05
    Verify Safety & Function

    Perform calibration, functional verification and applicable safety checks before release.

  6. 06
    Document & Return

    Record the completed work and communicate the equipment’s status before returning it to clinical service.
