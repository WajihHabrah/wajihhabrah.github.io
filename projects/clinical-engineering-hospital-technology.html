Medical Equipment Service Workflow

The exact procedure depends on the equipment and clinical situation, but a typical service intervention follows a structured and traceable workflow.

  1. 01
    Receive & Review

    Review the reported issue, equipment status, service history and relevant clinical context.

  2. 02
    Assess & Prioritize

    Evaluate technical risk and clinical urgency, and determine whether the equipment should remain out of service.

  3. 03
    Investigate

    Perform inspection, measurements and functional testing to identify the fault or maintenance requirement.

  4. 04
    Service

    Complete the required preventive maintenance, adjustment or corrective technical action.

  5. 05
    Verify Safety & Function

    Perform calibration, functional verification and applicable safety checks before release.

  6. 06
    Document & Return

    Record the completed work and communicate the equipment’s status before returning it to clinical service.

Mobile C-Arm Maintenance & Documentation

My clinical-engineering work included preventive maintenance, troubleshooting and repair of a broad range of Ziehm mobile C-arm systems, alongside C-arms from other manufacturers.

Documentation Need

Practical Maintenance Guidance

For Ziehm systems, the available service information did not always provide sufficient practical detail for completing a specific maintenance task consistently.

My Contribution

Illustrated How-To Instructions

I updated the existing preventive-maintenance documentation with illustrated, step-by-step guidance showing how the relevant task should be performed.

Broader Repair Work

Corrective C-Arm Repairs

My work on other mobile C-arm systems also included substantial corrective repairs and troubleshooting of faults involving loose components and main cable connections.

Result

More Repeatable Servicing

The updated guidance supported more consistent inspections, clearer documentation and traceability, and more repeatable preventive-maintenance work.
